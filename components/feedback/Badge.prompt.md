Small status or category pill. Soft tones by default; `solid` for filled.

```jsx
<Badge>Residential</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" dot>Insured</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" leadingIcon={<Icon name="map-pin" size={12} />}>Mount Vernon</Badge>
<Badge tone="brand" solid>Most popular</Badge>
```

- Tones: `brand | neutral | success | warning | danger`.
