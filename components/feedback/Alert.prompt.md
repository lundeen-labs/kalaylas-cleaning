Inline callout for status messages and reassurances (booking confirmed, what to expect).

```jsx
<Alert tone="success" title="You're booked!">We'll text you the day before your visit.</Alert>
<Alert tone="info">Free quotes, no obligation. Most replies within an hour.</Alert>
```

- Tones `info | success | warning | danger` set color and a default Lucide icon; override with `icon`.
